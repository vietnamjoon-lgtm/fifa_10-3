# Human rebuild sources

- Body, deformation hierarchy and original weights: MakeHuman hm08 core assets, CC0. See MAKEHUMAN-CC0.md. Generated by tools/build-human.mjs from the checked-in source; facial muscle bones are excluded. No EA/Nexon models or likeness photographs were added.
- Motion: Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab, subjects 09, 10 and 16. See CMU-MOCAP.txt. Source motion is retargeted, contact-tagged and resampled offline; no additional runtime dependencies.
- Dual quaternion implementation and corrective shapes: original project code informed by the mathematical method in Kavan et al., *Geometric Skinning with Approximate Dual Quaternion Blending* (2008): https://users.cs.utah.edu/~ladislav/kavan08geometric/kavan08geometric.pdf . No third-party implementation was copied.
- Running reference: Weyand et al. (2000), *Faster top running speeds are achieved with greater ground forces not more rapid leg movements*, https://doi.org/10.1152/jappl.2000.89.5.1991 . The paper supports distinguishing contact and swing duration; it does not certify the game's animation or the user document's entire numerical table.
- The default comparison athlete is fictional. The approved AI concept image is a design reference and is not shipped as evidence of actual rendering.
