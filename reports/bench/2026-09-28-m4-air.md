Reading: about 5 ms headroom at 60 Hz on M4. Largest costs are players (all 22 drawn in main and shadow passes;
`frustumCulled=false` in `src/human-body.js`), the shadow map (shadow pass plus 17-tap PCF sampling on the pitch)
and pixel count (retina ×1.5). The crowd is half of all triangles for a few pixels per spectator.
Removals overlap, so the savings do not add up. Weaker Windows laptops are where these costs will show as fps.
